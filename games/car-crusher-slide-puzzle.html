About this game

Car Crusher 3D is a Fun and Addictive 3D Puzzle Game. Engage Your Intellect and Unlock Your Brain with Board Puzzles. With its unique blend of color-matching puzzles and sliding mechanics, Car Crusher offers a refreshing and engaging twist on classic puzzle blocks. Slide the cars to unpuzzle them. But the puzzle blocks will only go away according to their directions and colors, so you’ve got to approach this swiping game and brain teaser carefully! There are multiple difficulty levels of puzzles to solve. Help the color blocks escape! Features: - 1100+ puzzles - Simple to play, move the blocks. - No time limit, block puzzle anytime. - Game offline, no internet connection required. - This game is designed for both kids and adults. Unblock the car and that is how you play this epic free puzzle game. Not only to kill time but also to boost up your logic skills. This Slide Puzzle is similar to other popular IQ brain test puzzle games like Unblock Me, Rush Hour, Parking Jam, Traffic Jam but with a unique twist. Whether you're a fan of logic games, board games, escape games, unblock games, slide puzzle or just looking for a fun way to pass the time, car crusher is the game for you! Download now and embark on your car crushing adventure!
