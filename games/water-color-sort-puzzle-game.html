About this game

Water Color Sort is a fun and interesting addictive puzzle game! Try to sort the colored water in the bottles until all colors are in the same bottle. A challenging yet relaxing game to exercise your brain! If you’d like to train your sorting logic, this water sort puzzle game is just for you! It’s the most relaxing and challenging puzzle game, and it’s not timed. How To Play Water Color Sort Game: • Tap any water bottle to pour water into another bottle. • The way to pour is that you can only pour the water if it is the same color and there’re enough space on the glass bottle. • To complete the level, one bottle must have single color only. • If you get stuck, you can add one more bottle to make it easier. • Use Undo to go back to the previous actions. Features: • Completely FREE to play • Play with ONE finger only • Multiple game play THEME • Multiple bottles SHAPE • No TIME bound • UNLIMITED levels! • UNIQUE & user-friendly interface • Free and CHALLENGING game • Water Sort Puzzle is a great game for the whole FAMILY to play together. Get ready for more exciting levels of color water games and test your intelligence by solving this color sort puzzle game.
