About this game

Word Stacks the daily word puzzle! Word game is in your pocket now, where you need to create words from the letters. Playing Word Stacks 10 mins a day sharpens your mind and prepares you for your daily life and challenges! ➤ Word Stacks is a word search - puzzle game, search and connect the right letters to get the correct word. Take on daily brain teasers and train your mind with our fun word games. ➤ Relax your brain with natural background and calm music. Take your daily medication and ease your mind while enjoying all the fun from Word Stacks! ➤ Word Stacks is a creative crossword puzzle game which can inspire your passion for brain challenges. HOW TO PLAY ● Swipe to connect hidden words in the right order and bring the Word Stacks crashing down! ● Use the Spyglass, Light bulb, or shuffle when you get stuck ● Get more hints with coins by purchasing or watching videos FEATURES ● Play over 2200 levels with more coming soon ● Each puzzle shifts as you find words. Improve your vocabulary and learn new words with Word Stacks.
