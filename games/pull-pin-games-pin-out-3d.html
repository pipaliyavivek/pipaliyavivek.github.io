About this game

The ultimate brain-teasing Pull Pin Games that will challenge your strategic thinking and problem-solving skills. In this exciting game, you are presented with a series of puzzles where you must remove the pins in the correct order to progress to the next level. Remove the pins in the right order and go to next level. But beware of bombs! Bomb explosion will destroy tanks and balls - one wrong move and the game is over! Wait there is another level of trickery! Sometimes some of the balls are colorless, before they go into the pipe, they need to touch a color ball, so the color spreads to them as well. You'll need to use your wits and strategic thinking to anticipate the consequences of your actions and make the right choices to emerge victorious. So what are you waiting for? Download Pin Out 3D today and start challenging your mind like never before!
