About this game

Control the assassin heroes and hunt down your targets one by one. Use your surroundings and shadows to stay hidden from flashlights. Targets with automatic robot, machine guns & weapons can be lurking around each corner. Attack them swiftly and escape without being spotted. Each eliminated target drops valuable gems. Use them to unlock and upgrade new heroes and weapons. Wage war in stunning fun 3D mini battles with your hunter heroes; equipped with guns, sniper rifles, swords, energy cannons and more! Hunter Heroes is designed exclusively to deliver premium gameplay experience in a Battle Royale.
