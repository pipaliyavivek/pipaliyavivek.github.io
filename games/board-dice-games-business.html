About this game

Fun Classic Board Games to play offline, Roll the Dice and be the Business Tycoon. Experience classic board game! Collect Properties, build Houses and Hotels, pull Chance Cards, and of course, earn that MONOPOLY Money! Play with business game in of buy and sell real estate properties. Earn more tokens as you go! Time for Business fun with Friends and Family! Wheel and deal as you build your fortune playing an exciting game of Business offline. Buy up whole neighborhoods, charge rent, and grow your empire. It's all about making deals and making money. But don't land in Jail! HOW TO PLAY Choose your player mode Classic Hasbro board game is a great game for 2 - 6 players. Put your landlord skills to the test against our challenging AI opponents and be a property tycoon in single player mode. Compete with friends and family. Play WIFI-free when you pass & play one device around a group of players. Build your property empire Roll the dice, take investment risks, bid for properties in auctions, make your way around the board and BUY or TRADING real estate, COLLECT rent, and DEVELOP houses, hotels, parking and lands to become a property tycoon. Players collect rent or fees from their opponents, with the goal being to drive them into bankruptcy. Money can also be gained or lost through Chance and Community Chest cards, and tax squares. But wait Players can end up in jail, which they cannot move from until they have met one of several conditions. There are other places on the board which can not be bought, but instead require the player to draw a card and perform the action on the card, pay taxes, collect income, or even go to jail. Any suggestions? We always love to hear from you for making this game better. Enjoy playing Business!!!
