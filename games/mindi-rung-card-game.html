About this game

Mindi is the Most Popular, Traditional, Time Passing game know in India. That is easy to learn and offer a unique game experience every time you play it. It is a team game and the ultimate objective is to win a maximum no. of 10 numbered cards for your team and complete as many coats against the opponents. In Mindi Coat, players must use strategy and quick thinking to win rounds and ultimately beat their opponents. The game is played with a standard deck of cards, with the objective being to get rid of all your cards before anyone else. Two Modes Of Mindi Card Game Hide Mode - In Mindi the player to the dealer's right selects a card placing it to table face down which will be declared as trump suit for that play. Katte Mode - Play begins without choosing a trump suit when the player is unable to follow suit then whichever he/she chooses becomes the trump of the deal. But that's just the beginning - Rung Card Game offers a variety of exciting gameplay modes that will keep you engaged for hours on end. Whether you're a seasoned pro or a newcomer to the game, there's something for everyone in Mindi. Compete against friends and strangers from around the world in the online multiplayer mode, or challenge yourself in the single-player mode against a range of computer opponents. With daily challenges and rewards, you'll always have something to look forward to in Mindi. The game also features stunning graphics and smooth gameplay, with intuitive touch controls that make it easy to play on any mobile device. So what are you waiting for? Download Mindi Card Game today and join the millions of players who are already hooked on this addictive and fun card game!
