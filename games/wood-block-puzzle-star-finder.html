About this game

Wood Block Puzzle Star Finder is an exciting line completion game using colorful wooden blocks of a wide variety of shapes. Use the wooden blocks to complete lines horizontally and vertically or 3x3 squares. Lines disappear upon completion. Simple easy to play, Match blocks to complete lines and cubes to get them removed. Keep the board clean and beat your high score in a Block Puzzle! Test your IQ and win the puzzle game! This simple block game will test your spatial intelligence and geometric skills! Block puzzle improve your logic skills and refreshes your mind. 🌼 How To Play Star Finder 1. Drag the given blocks and put them into the grid board 2. Obliterate the blocks by forming full lines horizontally or vertically or 3x3 squares! 3. Rotate the blocks by using power 4. Remove multiple lines at a time to score higher! 🌼 Block Puzzle Game Features: • No Internet? No problem! Enjoy anytime, anywhere! • Free to play! • Complete daily challenges and get more reward • No penalties & time limits! play at your own pace! • Stunning graphics! Catchy sound effects with mesmerizing visuals Challenge yourself and train your brain with block puzzle game. Let’s play Star Finder!
