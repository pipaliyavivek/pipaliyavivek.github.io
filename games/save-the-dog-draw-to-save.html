About this game

Test your brain and your drawing skills with Save The Doge - Draw To Save. It is a fun puzzle draw games. Hey! These bees are dangerous! Can you save the dog from the angry bees? You draw lines with your fingers to create walls that protect the dog from attacks by bees. You need to protect the dog with the your wall for 10 seconds during the attack of the bees, hold on and you will win the game. Use your brain to save the doge. Use less ink to get higher rate! Draw games help you train your brain effectively. You must use your freehand drawing skills to save a dog from a swarm of bees in the entertaining and brainteaser game Dog Rescue. Save your little dog from bees now. Try to win this entertaining game to sharpen your brain! Download and Draw now!
