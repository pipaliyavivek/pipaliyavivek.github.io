About this game

Cube Tile is a stylish match 3d puzzle game. You can swipe to rotate the cube match identical tiles to clear levels and use power to help you clear all tiles even more quickly. Tap and match triple tiles to progress to the next cube puzzle! Cube Tile Match 3D is a challenging puzzle game about matching three tiles together for both kids and adults! Gameplay: -> Swipe to rotate 3D cube -> Choose 3 same 3D tiles -> DON'T fill up collection bar -> Clear all tiles within limited time -> If stuck use Hint & shuffle boosters Cube Tile Match 3D Features: -> Addictive and sometimes needs a strategy -> Full angle rotation with the cool cube -> Hundreds of 3D tiles and shapes like animals, plane, cool toys, food, cars, fox, fruits... -> Train Brain and finger by matching triple tiles -> The best time killer when you want to relax -> Improves brain functions like memory, focus, attention, and concentration Cube Tile Match 3D is absolutely an addictive strategy games that you must fall in love with once you play it. As a board game, this matching 3d game is simple & entertaining for anybody to enjoy! Try the free triple matching puzzle now! Level up and grow to be a master in finding & matching elements.
