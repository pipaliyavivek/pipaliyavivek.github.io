About this game

Triple Tile Match 3D is a new and beautiful free matching game and enjoy awesome levels and fun challenges that will entertaining for all ages! By finding & matching 3D items in a limited time, you can power up your brain and speed up your thinking process. Hundreds of cute and diversified 3d combinations are waiting for you! Solve 3D tile puzzles. Clear the board by matching 3 tiles. Unlock beautiful chapters with the help of boosters. Improve your sorting skills to become a matching master! Spend a relaxing, fun time as you find and connect tiles and complete different missions. Looking for a fun way to enjoy a long car ride? Why not spend your time playing a cool Zen match game? Grab this amazing triple tile - mahjong puzzle from the store and start searching for hidden objects today! How To Play Triple Tile Match 3D: - Pick up three same 3d object from a pile of messed-up items and eliminate them. - Pay attention to the collecting bar, do not fill it up, or you'll fail the game. - Use boosters to help you quickly finish the level when necessary. - Keep doing that until you clear the whole screen & win the level. - Then keep having fun & start a new level. Match 3D Game Features: - Unique 3D game with beautiful tiles. - Easy to play. You just need to drag & tap! Addictive and sometimes needs a strategy. - animals, plane, cool toys, food, cars, fox, fruits... in different shapes & colors! - As you go further in the journey of being a master in matching 3D items, you can unlock new items one after another, which will definitely surprise you! - HUNDREDS OF PUZZLES: Well-designed brain trainer levels, beautiful 3D elements. - The best time killer when you want to relax. - Suits for all ages. You can play it with kids, friends, parents... anywhere & anytime you like! -…
