About this game

Traffic Jam: Car Escape is a challenging 3D puzzle game. Tap to move cars and escape from the traffic jam! Traffic escape - Move all cars from road and go next level. But be careful, don't hit! Just choose the right car to move, so take careful planning and strategic thinking. Challenge yourself in thousands of traffic jams with car game. Unlock all the challenging levels. Improve your critical thinking by solve traffic jam quickly and smoothly without crash. Challenge over 3000+ levels, and more. Colorful scenes, decorate your parking lot. Unlock cool cars and fill up your garage. No time limit, no internet, just unblock cars and relax in this car escape. Are you ready to test your puzzle-solving skills with Car Escape?
