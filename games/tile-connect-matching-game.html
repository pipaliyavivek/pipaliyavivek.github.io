About this game

Connect tiles with a great number of pictures in pairs within the time limit. As you crush all the tiles on the board, you can win the level! Become a tile master level by level! Play faster and faster to train your brain with the link puzzle Game! Ready to fully enjoy the collection of various pictures on the tiles: Fresh Fruits, Cute Animals, Delicious Cakes, Cool Vehicles, Beautiful Clothes, Lovely Toys, etc. Hundreds of new exciting levels are added every week! Come to match and crush tiles in the Mahjong puzzle game! Key Features of Tile Connect - Puzzle - Various pictures on the tiles: Thousands of pictures randomly appear level by level! Crush them as many as you can! Match fruit pairs, link animal pairs, connect number pairs... - Easy to play: Tap the tiles and connect them as fast as you can! - Play at anytime and anywhere! No matter you place, Tile Connect helps you relax and exercise! - Tile Match is one type of educational game. This free game will power up your brain and increase your memory speed since it offers tons of sweet and cute combinations. You can experience and enjoy with tile connect - brain game (mind game), which is simple to operate and can exercise your brain teaser. How to play Tile Connect Puzzle? - Find two identical tiles without the block of other tiles! Look carefully at what pictures it is. - Tap the tiles to connect with maximum three straight lines! More than three will not be accepted. - Use Power Search: Help you find one pair! Exchange: Help you change the matrix of the tiles! Changing pictures: Help you change the whole category of tiles! - Crush all the tiles within the limited time!
