About this game

Relax your mind, relieve your stress. Tap a cube to move it by the arrow direction. Move all cubes to complete the level. But the blocks will only go in one direction, so make careful planning and strategic thinking. As you progress, the blocks form different and bigger shapes. Swipe to rotate the shape and choose your next move. You cannot move a cube if the front of it has another cube. Unlock all the challenging levels. No time limits to release the box. Customize your color bricks with different skins and themes. Relax your brain, perfect time killer. Challenging yet stress relieving and satisfying Puzzle Game!
