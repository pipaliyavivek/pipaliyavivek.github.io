About this game

Get ready to take your game to the next level with Basketball Battle, the exciting sports game. Shoot hoops in 3 Point basketball contest! Your mission is simple: Throw the ball upper. Take it from above the basket and throw into the basket. It’s time to show off your basketball shooting skills! Each level comes with 3 stars you must complete first to advance to the next level. Hit the opponent to stop him/her throwing the ball into the basket. Basketball games offline, play anytime and anywhere. Basket Hoops delivers an immersive and exciting experience that will keep you fun. Train yourself anywhere and anytime with basketball legends games. Provides you the basketball battle experience. Take a deep breath and shoot for the hoop - just like in real life, feel like a professional basketball player, feel the pressure, feel the fun... and become a basketball star! Are you ready?
