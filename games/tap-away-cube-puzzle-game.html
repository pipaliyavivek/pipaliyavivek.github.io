About this game

The ultimate TAP Challenge is here! Tap the blocks to make clear the screen. But the blocks will only go in one direction, so make careful planning and strategic thinking. As you progress, the blocks form different and bigger shapes. Swipe to rotate the shape and choose your next move. Customize your blocks with different colorful skins and themes. Relax your brain, perfect time killer. Tricky, challenging yet stress relieving and satisfying Cube Puzzle Game!
