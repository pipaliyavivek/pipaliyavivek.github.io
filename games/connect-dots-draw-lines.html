About this game

Draw the line to connect the same color dots. Connect all dots, and cover the entire board to solve puzzle. But watch out, pipes will break if they cross or overlap! You'll need to use your strategic skills to plan the pipe placements carefully to avoid any breaks in the connection. Enjoy 2000+ free puzzles in color game. Play Three game modes Classic, Advance and Challenge. Clean, colorful graphics with relaxing sound effects. There are also daily challenges to test your skills and earn rewards. Connect Dots : Draw Lines is the perfect game to unwind and de-stress after a long day. You can compete against your friends on the leaderboard, or simply enjoy the satisfying feeling of completing each level on your own. So why wait? Download Connect dots - color game now and see if you have what it takes to master all the puzzles!
