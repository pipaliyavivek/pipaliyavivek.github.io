About this game

Find the differences between the two similar pictures and solve the puzzle! Easy and relaxing puzzle game. Focus on the different details, improve your powers of observation, and enjoy this free spot the difference game! HOW TO PLAY: - Compare two pictures to spot the differences. - Spot the difference and tap on it to highlight the different objects. - Pinch the image to zoom in to see more details. - Click the hint button when you get stuck in puzzle game & need help to spot the differences. FEATURES: - Simple and intuitive game design. - Thousands of High-quality pictures are waiting for you to challenge. - A Brain Training game for all ages & interests, anywhere & anytime. - Enjoy thousands of levels and experience the joy of concentration.
